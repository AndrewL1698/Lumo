Round 2

Luci, built out

The name carries more than it seems to at first. Below: why it works, a glow system that maps to the product's real word states, a few alternates, and Luci's glowworm stage at the end.

Lucía · "Luci" for short

Read it. Say it. Light it up.

  • A firefly: luciérnaga, the bug that glows on your page.
  • A real name: Lucía is one of the most common girls' names in the Spanish-speaking world, so the tutor has a name, not a brand.
  • A verb: lucía means "it shone." The word itself is past tense of glowing.
  • A reading saint: Santa Lucía is the patron saint of eyes and sight. Fitting for an app that learns from what you read.
  • The root is light: lux, luz. One-liner for judges: "Luci sheds light on the words you're ready for."
The clever part

Glow brightness is the learning state

Every swapped word on the page glows, and how bright it glows tells you how well you know it. This makes the evidence rule a visible feature instead of a hidden one.

I walked into the cocina and found the old mesa still covered in letters from my abuela.
Seen: a faint glow Learning: it flickers Known: a steady light

"Luci can't light it for you. You light it by saying it out loud."

This matches the spec exactly: hovering can make a word flicker (learning), but only a quiz pass or correct use in speech makes it glow steady (known).

The rest of the system

Names for every screen

Your jar

el frasco: the vocabulary page. Catching fireflies in a jar is a childhood memory everyone has. Each known word is a firefly in it.

Sparks

chispas: the words swapped on today's pages. "Luci left 8 sparks on this article."

Night session

The voice tutor. Read by day, and the fireflies come out at night for the conversation. Matches the pitch line word for word.

Lights out

The privacy pause. One click and Luci goes dark on this site. Never-list sites are "always dark."

Other directions

If you want to keep looking

Foco

FOH-ko · lightbulb (Latin America)

In English it reads as "focus." In Mexico, se me prendió el foco means "the lightbulb went on," the moment you get it.

Pairs with Luci: the firefly is the mascot, Foco the moment a word clicks.

Chispa

CHEES-pa · spark

Tener chispa means to be quick and witty. Your Spanish gets its spark.

Strong as the name for swapped words, a little generic as the product name.

Beelingual

a bee · English pun

The bee gathers words from every page you visit and turns them into honey, which is fluency. Plus "spelling bee."

The pun lands instantly with English-speaking judges, but it's a pun many apps have tried.

Tela

TEH-la · web, and fabric

A small spider weaving words into the World Wide Web. Tela de araña is a spider web.

Very clever about the browser, but spiders aren't cute to everyone.

Oruga

oh-ROO-ga · caterpillar

Each word starts as a caterpillar and becomes a mariposa once you say it. The progress meter is a metamorphosis.

Good mechanic, weaker name. The glowworm below brings this idea into Luci.

Debug

a bug that catches bugs

The bug in your browser finds the bugs in your Spanish. Programmers at a hackathon will smile.

Funny for this room, but it says "coding," not "language."

Luci's first stage

The glowworm

Before Luci flies, she crawls.

Firefly larvae are called glowworms, and they look like small glowing caterpillars. So Luci starts life as a glowworm and grows into a firefly. That puts the Oruga idea inside Luci without changing the name.

the old mesa was still there

The glowworm is the underline. It crawls under a swapped word, and the word lights up behind it. The extension already needs some kind of underline for swapped words, so this mascot costs almost nothing to build.
